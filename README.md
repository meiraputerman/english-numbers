# Numbers by Ear

Practise English numbers by ear. The game says a number in English and you type it in digits as fast as you can. The quicker you answer, the more points you get. Made for Hebrew, French and Ukrainian speakers learning English; a sister game to [À l'oreille](https://meiraputerman.github.io/french-numbers/).

## What you can practise

| Mode | What you hear |
|---|---|
| 0 to 20, 0 to 99 | Whole numbers |
| Teens and tens | Thirteen or thirty? Fourteen or forty? |
| Hundreds, Thousands | 100 to 999, and 1,000 to 999,999 |
| Years | "nineteen eighty-five" (type 1985) |
| Full dates | "July fourteenth, seventeen eighty-nine" (US, type 07/14/1789) or "the fourteenth of July, seventeen eighty-nine" (UK, type 14/07/1789) |
| Prices | "twelve dollars and fifty cents" or "twelve pounds fifty" (type 12.50) |
| Phone numbers | Said digit by digit; in British English with "oh" and "double seven" |
| Mix | A bit of everything |

## How to play

- Answer by typing, by picking from 3 to 6 choices, or by saying the number out loud in English, Hebrew, French or Ukrainian.
- Or turn it around with "Saying it in English": the number is shown and you say it in English (or write it in English words when there's no microphone).
- Choose American or British English in Settings. It changes the voice, the date order, the currency and how phone numbers are said.
- The game can be shown in English, Hebrew, French or Ukrainian: Settings › Language · שפה · Langue · Мова.
- A wrong answer doesn't give the number away: you hear it again and try another answer, but that number scores no points.
- Skip shows the answer and moves on.
- A fast first-try answer scores up to 100 points, less for each second you take and each time you tap Hear again, plus a streak bonus of up to 50.

## What you need

- Chrome, on Android or a computer. The numbers are spoken by your device's text-to-speech.
- For spoken answers, allow the microphone when Chrome asks.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser, and settings and best scores are saved only there. If you answer by voice, Chrome may send the recording to Google's speech service to turn it into text.

## Files

`index.html` is the whole game: one file, no build step, no server.
